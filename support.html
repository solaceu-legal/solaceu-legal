Lightvessel Support

For help with Lightvessel, email support@lenia.tech. Please include your app version, iOS version, device model, and a description of the issue. Do not include private conversation content unless it is necessary and you choose to share it.

Subscriptions

Restore a purchase from Lightvessel → Settings → Restore Purchases. Manage or cancel a subscription from iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

Account and data

Erase local content from Lightvessel → Settings → Clear All Data. Permanently delete your account and associated server data from Lightvessel → Settings → Delete Account. Account deletion does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Privacy and safety

Privacy requests: privacy@lenia.tech. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Lightvessel is not an emergency or medical service. If you may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or find a regional crisis service at findahelpline.com.
