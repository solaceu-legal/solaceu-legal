06WHY “LIGHTVESSEL”为什么叫 LIGHTVESSEL

Not a lighthouse on a distant shore — a ship anchored beside you. 不是远处岸上的灯塔，是一艘停泊在你身边的灯船。

A lightvessel is a real thing: a ship that carries a lighthouse’s light, anchored in open water where it’s needed most. It doesn’t steer anyone’s ship. It doesn’t ask the sea to be calm. It simply stays — visible, steady, close — for anyone who needs a place to pause. 灯船是真实存在的东西：一艘自带灯塔之光的船，锚泊在最需要它的海面上。它不替任何人掌舵，也不要求海面平静，只是停在那里——可见、稳定、很近——给需要停一停的人。

07QUIET BY DESIGN把安心放在心上

Your records
stay in your hands. 你的记录，
由你掌控。

Companionship needs trust. Your records stay on your device, and the app explains when text is sent to generate an AI reply. 陪伴需要信任。你的记录保存在设备上；生成 AI 回复时，相关内容会安全传输处理。

On your device by default记录留在设备上

Your conversations, mood records, memories, and bottles are stored on your iPhone. To generate AI replies, relevant text and context are sent securely to our AI provider for processing; we don’t keep ordinary conversations on our servers.对话、情绪记录、记忆和漂流瓶保存在你的 iPhone 上。生成 AI 回复时，相关文字和上下文会通过安全连接发送给 AI 服务处理；我们不会在自己的服务器上保存普通对话。

No ads. No tracking.没有广告，没有追踪

No advertising or tracking SDKs. Your personal data is not sold. You can clear your local records or delete your account in the app.没有广告或追踪 SDK，也不出售个人数据。你可以在应用内清除本地记录或删除账号。

Choose what it remembers由你掌控的记忆

Review saved memories one by one in the Memory tab. Choose any item to forget it.记忆页会逐条列出保存的内容，你可以选择忘记其中任何一条。

Honest about its limits诚实说明边界

Is Lightvessel a therapist?Lightvessel 是心理治疗师吗？+

No. It’s an AI companion for reflection and everyday emotional support. It doesn’t diagnose or replace professional care, and it can make mistakes.不是。Lightvessel 用于日常倾诉和梳理，回复由 AI 生成，可能出错。它不提供诊断，也不能替代专业帮助。
