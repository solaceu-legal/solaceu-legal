The horizon has been quiet this week, with no particular marks left on the sand. Sometimes the water just drifts in silence, not needing to crash against the shore or pull back to prove it’s moving. You don’t have to keep a log of the waves to acknowledge they exist; even when the tide feels still, the ocean is holding its own depth.

If you find yourself wandering down to the water’s edge next week, try simply picking up a stone that catches your eye—just to feel its weight and temperature, and nothing more. The light here remains steady, regardless of the quiet.

06WHY “LIGHTVESSEL”为什么叫 LIGHTVESSEL

Not a lighthouse on a distant shore — a ship anchored beside you. 不是远处岸上的灯塔，是一艘停泊在你身边的灯船。

A lightvessel is a real thing: a ship that carries a lighthouse’s light, anchored in open water where it’s needed most. It doesn’t steer anyone’s ship. It doesn’t ask the sea to be calm. It simply stays — visible, steady, close — for anyone who needs a place to pause. 灯船是真实存在的东西：一艘自带灯塔之光的船，锚泊在最需要它的海面上。它不替任何人掌舵，也不要求海面平静，只是停在那里——可见、稳定、很近——给需要停一停的人。
