04为什么叫 LightvesselWHY LIGHTVESSEL

不是远处的灯塔，
而是一艘与你同在的灯船。

Not a lighthouse far away,
a lightship here beside you.

Lightvessel 是“灯船”。它不要求海面平静，也不催你驶向某个终点。它带着一束稳定的光，停在心海边——当你需要的时候，可以靠过来，说一说，喘口气，再按自己的方向出发。

A lightvessel is a ship that carries its own light. It does not ask the sea to be calm or point you toward a finish line. It stays nearby—a steady light to come alongside when you want to talk, breathe, and find your own way forward.

“潮起潮落，你都可以在这里停一停。Through every tide, there is room to pause.
