LIGHTVESSEL FOR IPHONE

Keep a small record of how you feel.

It can be hard to remember how you felt last Tuesday, or what made a good day different. Lightvessel’s mood tracker lets you save a quick check-in on your iPhone and come back to it later.

Make your first check-in

  1. Choose the sea conditions that match your mood: storm, fog, sunshine, or something in between.
  2. Pick how your body feels. Add a note if there is something you want to remember.
  3. Save the entry. You will find it in the Tides tab.

“Tired after the meeting” or “Had lunch with a friend” is plenty. You can write more, leave the note blank, or pick it up again after a few days away.

Look back at your week

Tides shows your recent check-ins. After you have recorded a few days, a weekly letter can help you revisit them. You might notice that the same situation comes up often, or remember something you would like to do again. These are reflections on your entries, not a health assessment.

Talk about an entry

If a note brings up more than you want to write, open a conversation with Lightvessel. Choose Vent to talk freely, Tidy to work through a situation, or Stay for quiet company.

Where your records go

Mood entries are stored on your iPhone. When you use AI features, relevant recent entries may be sent with the conversation to help generate a reply. There are no advertising or tracking SDKs in the app. Read the Privacy Policy for the full details.

Try it on iPhone

Lightvessel includes mood check-ins and a limited number of free AI conversations. Premium unlocks unlimited conversations; prices and any available trial are shown in the App Store before purchase.

Lightvessel does not diagnose or treat health conditions. If you need help with a health concern, speak with a qualified professional.

If you would rather leave the entry for later, you can follow the lighthouse breathing or spend a moment on the Now screen.
