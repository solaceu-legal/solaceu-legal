Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-12 · Last updated: 2026-08-22

Lenia ("Lenia," "we," "us," or "our") provides an AI-powered emotional companionship app (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Lenia, you agree to the practices described here.

The short version: Your conversations and mood entries are stored on your device, not in a cloud account. We do not sell your data, we do not share it for advertising purposes, and we do not embed any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs in this App. Message content is sent to our server and our AI provider only to generate a response to you, and is not stored long-term unless you submit a Report.

1. Who we are

Lenia is developed and operated by WANG XIAOHANG. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at privacy@lenia.tech.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account information

When you sign in with "Sign in with Apple," Apple provides us with a unique, anonymized identifier for your account. We do not request or receive your name or email address through this process. This identifier is stored securely on your device (in the iOS Keychain).

2.2 Conversations and mood entries

The messages you send, the AI's replies, and your mood check-in entries are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not maintain a cloud copy of your conversation history or mood data at this time.

When you send a message, the text of that message (along with your recent conversation history, limited to your last 40 messages, to maintain context) is transmitted to our server and then to our AI language model provider (see Section 5) solely to generate a reply. This content is not permanently stored on our server. Our server also performs automated safety screening on your message, combining keyword detection with a lightweight secondary AI risk-assessment check, to detect possible signs of self-harm or crisis, in order to provide you with appropriate crisis resources. This screening happens transiently: the content is used only to make that determination and is not itself stored.

To help Lenia maintain continuity across your conversations, the App periodically generates a short "relationship summary" (for example, what you've shared about your situation, or what kinds of responses have been helpful to you). This summary is generated using our AI provider but is stored only on your device — it is never uploaded to or retained on our servers — and is included as context only in the requests your device sends to generate a reply.

2.3 Voice input

If you use voice input, your speech is processed using Apple's on-device Speech Recognition framework to convert it to text. Depending on your device and language settings, this processing may occur entirely on your device or, where on-device recognition is unavailable, may be sent to Apple's servers for transcription, subject to Apple's own privacy practices.

2.4 Voice output (text-to-speech)

If you choose to have an AI reply read aloud, the text of that reply is sent to Google LLC's Cloud Text-to-Speech service solely to generate the spoken audio, which is then played on your device. Google's handling of this data is governed by their own privacy practices.

2.5 Report submissions

If you use the "Report" feature to flag an AI reply, we collect and store the flagged message content, your selected reason, and any note you provide, in order to review the report and improve the App's safety systems. Report submissions are retained on our server for this purpose.

2.6 Subscription and purchase information

Subscriptions are processed entirely through Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive or store your payment information (such as card numbers). We only receive confirmation from Apple of your subscription status (active, expired, etc.) in order to unlock Premium features on your device.

2.7 Automatically collected technical information

Our server may log limited technical information for security and debugging purposes, such as timestamps and error details. We do not use this information to build advertising profiles.

3. Third-party SDKs and services

Lenia does not include any third-party advertising SDKs, analytics/tracking SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs. The only external frameworks the App uses are Apple's own first-party frameworks: Sign in with Apple (authentication), StoreKit (subscriptions), and the Speech framework (voice input) — each governed by Apple's own privacy practices, not a separate third-party vendor. The only outside service the App relies on is our own backend server's connection to our AI language model provider, described in Section 5 below.

4. How we use your information

  • To generate AI responses to your messages
  • To detect potential crisis situations and surface relevant support resources
  • To let you pick up your conversations and mood history on the same device
  • To verify your subscription status and provide Premium features
  • To review content you report, and to maintain the safety and integrity of the App
  • To maintain and secure our service (e.g., diagnosing technical problems)

We do not use your data to serve advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or "share" (as that term is defined under the CCPA) your personal information to third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising or any other purpose.

6. Sensitive information

Conversations with Lenia may reveal information about your mental or emotional health. Under laws like the CCPA/CPRA, this may be considered "sensitive personal information," and under GDPR, a "special category" of data. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we limit its transmission to what is necessary to provide the service described above. We do not use this information for any purpose beyond providing and improving the App's core functionality.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Conversations, mood entries, and your relationship summary stored on your device remain there until you delete them using the "Clear All Conversations" option in Settings, or delete the App entirely.
  • Report submissions are retained on our server for as long as reasonably necessary for safety review, and are periodically reviewed for deletion.
  • To delete your account and locally stored data, delete the App from your device. To request deletion of any Report data associated with your account, contact us at privacy@lenia.tech.

8. Your privacy rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Port your data to another service in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

8.2 If you are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose
  • Delete personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or "sharing" of personal information — we do not sell or share your data, so there is nothing to opt out of, but you retain this right
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information — we already limit our use of it to providing the App's core functionality, as described in Section 6
  • Non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights

8.3 Exercising your rights

To exercise any of the above rights, contact us at privacy@lenia.tech. We will verify your request and respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You may also exercise many of these rights directly by deleting the App (which removes your on-device data) or by using in-App settings.

9. International data transfers

Because our service providers operate in different countries, your information may be processed in a country other than the one in which you live, which may have different data protection laws. Where required, we take steps to ensure such transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers from the EEA).

10. Children's privacy

Lenia is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, anyone under the age of 17 (or the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, if higher). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Not a substitute for professional care

Lenia is an AI companion for emotional support and is not a medical device, and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or advice. It cannot replace care from a licensed physician or mental health professional. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis helpline immediately; you can find one at findahelpline.com.

12. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections between the App and our servers, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
