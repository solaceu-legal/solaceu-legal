LIGHTVESSEL FOR IPHONE

A place to talk through your day.

A conversation keeps replaying in your head. Work followed you home. Or you just want to say how the day went. Lightvessel is an AI companion on iPhone where you can start with whatever is on your mind.

Vent, Tidy, or Stay

Vent is for getting things off your chest. Start with “I had a rough day” or tell the whole story.

Tidy helps you work through one situation. A couple of questions lead to a clarity card you can keep, with a summary and a small next step.

Stay is for quiet company. Use it when you want a conversation without having to explain everything. You can also leave the conversation for later and follow the lighthouse breathing, or just spend a moment on the Now screen.

For examples of what you could say in each mode, read how to choose Vent, Tidy, or Stay.

Keep a note of how you feel

You can also record your mood and add a short note. The Tides tab brings those check-ins together so you can look back at your week.

You choose what it remembers

Open the Memory tab to see saved details. To remove one, tap Forget and confirm. Your conversation history and mood records stay on your iPhone. Messages and relevant context are sent to the AI service to generate replies. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Before you start

Replies come from AI and may be wrong. Lightvessel is for everyday conversation and reflection; it does not provide therapy, diagnosis, or emergency support. In immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

You can try a limited number of AI conversations for free. Premium unlocks unlimited conversations. The App Store shows your price, renewal terms, and any available trial before you subscribe.
